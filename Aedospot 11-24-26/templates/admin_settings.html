Dengue Preparedness Warnings
Enable Trend Warnings
Show a Watch / Warning on the Alerts page when a reading stays inside its warning zone (set below) for several days in a row.
Notify Residents by Email
When a factor reaches the Warning level (the 4th day in its zone and above, by default), send the warning Email message once per streak to residents near that device. Each resident gets it only if they enabled Email in Residents, after their own delay (15/30/60 min). It is cancelled if the streak drops back to Watch or the device turns High risk, and is never sent together with a High risk notification.
Watch After (days in a row)
Amber "Watch" appears when a parameter stays inside its warning zone for this many consecutive days. Day 1 gives no alert, so the default is day 2 (days 2 and 3 are Watch).
Warning After (days in a row)
Red "Warning" appears from this consecutive day onward and the email goes out to residents — by default the 4th day and every day after it. Must not be lower than the Watch days.
Warning Zones (range per parameter)
A day counts toward the streak when the day's reading falls between the minimum and maximum below (both included). Untick a parameter to stop warning about it. The reason behind each default is explained under "About the Warning Zones" below.

About the Warning Zones
How Watch and Warning are decided
Each parameter (temperature, humidity, rainfall, wind speed) is checked on its own, using the device's daily readings. A day that falls inside the zone adds to the streak; one day outside the zone resets it to zero.
Recommended zones and why
These are the default zones that the "Restore recommended zones" button brings back. You can change them above to fit local conditions.
ParameterWarning zoneWhy this range
Temperature 23 – 28 °C This is the range with the highest chance of mosquito breeding. Eggs hatch quickly, larvae grow fast, and adult mosquitoes live longer and bite more often.
Humidity 77 – 80 % Warm, moist air keeps eggs from drying out and lets adult mosquitoes survive longer and stay active, which supports breeding and biting.
Rainfall 3 – 7 mm/day Light to moderate rain leaves water in containers, puddles and gutters, which mosquitoes use to lay eggs. Very heavy rain can wash larvae away, so the zone stops at 20 mm a day.
Wind Speed 0 – 1.6 m/s Mosquitoes fly and bite best when the air is calm. Stronger wind makes flying difficult for them, so calm to light air is the favorable zone.
Good to know. A Watch or Warning means current conditions favor mosquito breeding; it does not count dengue cases. Each parameter is checked on its own, so any one of them can trigger a Watch or Warning.
